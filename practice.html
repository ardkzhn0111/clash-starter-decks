A ROUTINE YOU CAN REPEAT

Prepare. Play. Review.

01 / PREPARE

Know your tools.

Find your tower attacker, your air defense and your cheap response. Read their roles before starting a match.

02 / PLAY

Watch one habit.

Pay attention to your chosen skill. Avoid judging a whole session by one win or one loss.

03 / REVIEW

Take one lesson.

Watch a replay and identify a specific moment you would play differently. Carry that lesson into your next session.
