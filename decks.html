Giant Beatdown

A steady push with a big front line.

  • 3 Archers
  • 3 Minions
  • 2 Bomber
  • 3 Arrows
  • 4 Fireball

THE GAME PLAN

Let the Giant take the hits.

Giant targets buildings. Put support behind him to clear defending troops while he walks toward the tower. Mini P.E.K.K.A handles enemy tanks; Musketeer and Minions help against air.

Avoid this: spending all your elixir behind one Giant. Keep enough to defend the other lane.

  1. 01
    Defend before you commit

    Use Mini P.E.K.K.A against a tank, or Archers and Bomber against suitable ground troops.

  2. 02
    Turn defense into a push

    Place Giant in front of surviving support troops. Add Musketeer if you need air coverage.

  3. 03
    Make room for your Giant

    Use Arrows on swarms. Save Fireball for valuable groups instead of an empty tower.

Hog 2.6 Cycle

Small defenses. Frequent attacks.

2.6 ♦ AVG. ELIXIR
  • 4 Hog Rider
  • 2 Ice Golem
  • 4 Musketeer
  • 3 Cannon
  • 1 Skeletons
  • 1 Ice Spirit
  • 4 Fireball
  • 2 The Log

THE GAME PLAN

Defend efficiently. Send the Hog.

Hog Rider targets buildings. Use low-cost cards to defend and return to your next Hog quickly. This classic deck rewards careful timing and placement, so expect a learning curve.

Avoid this: sending Hog Rider every time he is available. Save enough elixir for Cannon and Musketeer when needed.

  1. 01
    Keep your defense simple

    Pull building-targeting troops toward the middle with Cannon. Protect Musketeer for air defense.

  2. 02
    Attack with a small commitment

    Send Hog when you can afford it. Ice Golem can take hits in front; Ice Spirit can briefly freeze defenders.

  3. 03
    Cycle with a purpose

    Use Skeletons and Ice Spirit for useful distractions. Do not waste cards just to reach another Hog.

Standard cards only; no evolutions required. Card availability depends on your progress. Hog 2.6 includes the Legendary card The Log. These are learning decks, not a promise of wins.

BEFORE YOUR NEXT BATTLE

Three habits. A stronger start.

01 / ELIXIR

Leave room to defend.

A push is only useful if you can handle what comes back. Avoid spending all ten elixir at once.

02 / PRACTICE

Learn one deck first.

Keep the same eight cards for a few practice games. Notice which placements work before switching decks.

03 / REFLECT

Watch one mistake.

After a loss, check your replay. Find one wasted spell or late defense, then focus on that next game.
