YOUR FIRST FEW GAMES

One deck.
Three small steps.

You do not need to learn every card at once. Give yourself one useful goal each game.

  1. 01

    Choose a rhythm.

    A slower Giant push or a faster Hog cycle? Start with the one you understand, not the one that looks impressive.

  2. 02

    Practice one defense.

    Notice which card handles an enemy tank or an air troop. Good defense gives your next attack a better start.

  3. 03

    Review, then try again.

    Watch a replay and find one thing to improve. Change your timing before changing your whole deck.

BEFORE YOU START

A few quick answers.

Do I need every card before trying a deck?

To play the exact list, yes. Check which cards you have unlocked first. In particular, classic Hog 2.6 includes The Log, a Legendary card. This guide does not assume every card is available on your first day.

Which deck should I learn first?

Start with Giant Beatdown if you want a clearer attack plan: a tank in front and support behind. Try Hog 2.6 when you are ready to practice precise defense and a faster cycle.

Will a deck guarantee that I win?

No. Card levels, matchups, placement and timing all matter. These decks are starting points for learning, not a promise of wins or a ranking of the current meta.
