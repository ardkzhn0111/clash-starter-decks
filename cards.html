THINK IN ROLES

Build a team, not a pile of cards.

♜

Attack the tower.

Your main tower attacker gives the deck a plan. Here, that is Giant or Hog Rider. Help that card reach a building.

+

Support and defend.

Support troops clear the way and protect your towers. Include ways to answer both air and ground threats.

♦

Make space.

Spells clear groups. Cheap cards distract enemies and keep your deck moving. Every elixir spent should have a purpose.

THE 14-CARD GUIDE

Meet the cards in your decks.

Standard cards, without evolutions.
Costs are shown in purple.

5
Tower attacker

Giant

Targets buildings and takes hits for the troops behind him.

Try this: Place him in front of surviving support troops to begin a counterpush.

4
Tower attacker

Hog Rider

A fast building-targeting troop for repeated attacks.

Try this: Send him when you can still afford a defense, not just because he is in your hand.

4
Air & ground support

Musketeer

A ranged troop that can hit both air and ground targets.

Try this: Give her space behind a tank and keep her away from enemy melee troops.

4
Ground defense

Mini P.E.K.K.A

Strong single-target damage against ground troops.

Try this: Use against a tank; support him against swarms. He cannot hit air troops.

3
Air & ground support

Archers

Two ranged troops for flexible defense.

Try this: Use their range and protect them from spells or close-range attackers.

3
Air support

Minions

Flying troops that attack air and ground targets.

Try this: Help defend against troops that cannot hit air, but watch for enemy spells.

2
Ground splash

Bomber

Low-cost area damage against ground troops.

Try this: Clear clustered ground troops from behind a tank. He cannot hit air.

3
Defensive building

Cannon

Pulls building-targeting attackers and hits ground troops.

Try this: Place toward the middle to draw an attacker away from your tower. Add air defense separately.

2
Cheap tank

Ice Golem

A low-cost building-targeting troop that absorbs damage.

Try this: Use him in front of Hog or to lead suitable enemy troops away from your defenders.

1
Distraction

Skeletons

Cheap ground troops that buy time and help cycle your deck.

Try this: Distract a single-target attacker while a tower or support troop deals damage.

1
Brief control

Ice Spirit

A cheap jumping troop that briefly freezes enemies it hits.

Try this: Use its freeze to give your defense extra time or support an attack.

3
Area spell

Arrows

Wide-area damage to groups of air or ground troops.

Try this: Save for a swarm that threatens your push or your tower.

4
Area spell

Fireball

Area damage for valuable groups of troops.

Try this: Look for multiple useful targets. Avoid spending four elixir without a clear reason.

2
Ground spell

The Log

A rolling spell that damages and pushes back ground troops.

Try this: Clear small ground troops. It does not hit air, and it is a Legendary card.

BETTER TOGETHER

Two simple combinations to learn.

+

Giant + Musketeer

Giant takes the front position. Musketeer supports from behind and can answer air troops. Build this from a surviving defender when possible: together, these cards cost nine elixir.

See the full Giant deck ↗
+

Ice Golem + Hog Rider

Ice Golem goes in front and absorbs some damage while Hog heads for a building. This costs six elixir, so check that you can still defend before committing.
